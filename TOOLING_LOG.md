# Tooling Log

## ChatGPT Co-Founder Control Plane

The Vuka MCP now includes a ChatGPT co-founder control plane under `/api/mcp-cofounder`.

It provides durable company state, goals, decision/approval tracking, action auditability, research memory, campaign tracking, budget controls, and founder dashboard data. ChatGPT is the reasoning layer; the MCP does not call a second AI provider.

The campaign layer supports both the original Founding 15 experiment and a flexible Vuka Traction Lab with separate artist and friends/fan transaction tracks under a hard R3,000 ceiling.

Externally consequential actions remain approval-gated, and campaign commitments cannot exceed the configured hard budget or track allocation.
